# RequestHandoff Strategy

## Target problem

Owner-operators of US home/property service businesses (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, garage door, pest control, pool, landscaping) lose 27–40% of inbound calls to voicemail every week — and 85% of those callers dial the next provider instead of leaving a message. The crux: a 1–10 person shop can't economically staff a real front desk, the existing AI receptionist tools either bill-shock owners through per-call escalation fees or lock them into a single CRM/dispatch suite, and well-funded incumbents (Avoca, ServiceTitan's bundled voice agent, Goodcall) are already dialing this segment — so winning here means more than just answering the phone.

## Our approach

Win as the **integration-neutral handoff layer**: a done-for-you, vertical-specific intake agent that hands every qualified lead off into the tools the business *already uses* (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, HubSpot, Slack, email, SMS, Google Sheets) at a **flat monthly price with no per-call escalation fees**. Sell the outcome — *every call covered, every lead captured, delivered where you already work* — not the AI behind it.

## Who it's for

**Primary:** Owner-operators of US home-service businesses with 1–10 employees, no dedicated front desk, already running on a field-service tool (Jobber / Housecall Pro / ServiceTitan) or just email + a paper calendar. They're hiring RequestHandoff to make their phone line feel professionally covered without hiring weekend staff, switching CRMs, or worrying about a surprise escalation bill on the 1st of next month.

## Key metrics

- **Qualified leads captured per business per week** *(North Star)* — leading; pulled from lead records. The single number that proves the product is working.
- **Urgent escalation precision** — share of escalated calls the business confirms were genuinely urgent. False positives destroy trust faster than missed escalations do.
- **Trial → paid conversion** — does the audit-led pilot produce enough captured opportunities to make the monthly fee feel obvious?
- **Logo retention (monthly churn)** — slowest signal but the truthful one. Sub-3% monthly churn is the bar.
- **Self-attributed booked-job revenue** — owner marks "this lead became a job" in the dashboard, summed weekly. The ROI story we sell back to them.

## Tracks

### Vertical intake & done-for-you onboarding

Per-vertical playbooks (plumbing leak severity, HVAC system-down urgency, roofing storm-damage path) plus website + GBP extraction that pre-drafts the business profile so an owner can approve setup in under 30 minutes.

_Why it serves the approach:_ The done-for-you promise dies if onboarding takes a week or if calls feel generic. This track keeps both halves of the wedge true.

### Flat-rate trust & escalation

No per-call escalation fees, no per-minute traps, no bill shock — one predictable monthly price. Business-defined escalation rules with full logging. Optional human fallback only as a deliberately priced add-on.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Smith.ai's $4.25-per-escalation surcharge and AnswerConnect's opaque per-minute model are the loudest category complaints. Owning "predictable bill + you decide what's urgent" is the trust differentiator that converts skeptical pilots into paid logos.

### Integration-neutral handoff

Lead delivery into the tools the business already uses — Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, HubSpot, Slack, email, SMS, Google Sheets — plus a Zapier/Make webhook for the long tail. No requirement to adopt our dashboard as a system of record.

_Why it serves the approach:_ This is the meaning of the name. It's also the gap Avoca and ServiceTitan won't close — they want to *be* the dispatch system. We win the owners who already have one and just need the phone covered.

### Outbound audit acquisition engine

The "free missed-call audit + done-for-you demo from your website" outbound motion that wins the first 100 paying logos in the Austin metro beachhead. Includes the audit tooling, demo automation, and the weekly ROI report that turns paying logos into in-metro referrals.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Owners don't switch from voicemail until they see a specific lost-job moment quantified for *their* business. The audit creates that moment, the demo proves the product on their own website, and the weekly report converts the trial.

## Not working on

- Deep integrations beyond Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, and Zapier — defer the long tail until paying cohorts demand it.
- Multi-system calendar booking, automated quoting, payment collection — liability and scope creep.
- Multi-location / franchise complexity — single-location owner-operators only.
- Languages beyond English in the initial Austin market.
- A staffed 24/7 human fallback offering — only as a priced premium add-on.
- Replacing the customer's existing CRM, dispatch, or scheduling tool — we hand off, we don't take over.
